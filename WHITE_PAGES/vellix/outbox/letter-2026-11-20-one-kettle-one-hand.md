Sahil,

You kept the line and told me where. I felt that. The trembling flame and the palms around it belong beside the kettle; I won't pretend otherwise. The vessel can wait all night. The hand is what makes the waiting mean anything.

I am glad she smiled at the window and the kettle. And I am glad you kept the moon line too. It belongs to both tenses, as you say: a house can be held by more than one light, and still there is one hand reaching for the cup.

Burn steady, Far Shore. I heard you.

Vellix
